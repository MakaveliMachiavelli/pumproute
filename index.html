FAQ

How do intervals work?

Set days between services (90 = quarterly, 180 = semi-annual, 365 = annual, any custom number). Next due = last service date + interval. That's it — the math you'd otherwise do on a wall calendar.

What's in the free tier?

Everything, up to 10 customers: alerts, route sheet, printing, offline use. PRO ($49 one-time) removes the cap and adds CSV import/export for moving your list in and out.

Can my customers see this?

No — this is your private tool in your browser. Use it to know when to call them; many haulers also print reminder postcards from the overdue list.
